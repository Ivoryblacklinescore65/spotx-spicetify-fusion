## 📋 Frequently Asked Questions

### ❓ Is this safe to use?

Yes. Both SpotX and Spicetify are widely used open-source projects with thousands of users. The fusion installer simply combines them and applies compatibility fixes. Nothing is sent to external servers — all processing happens locally on your computer.

### ❓ Will this break my Spotify account?

No. Your account stays the same. The installer only modifies the Spotify desktop application files on your computer, not your account information. You can still log in normally, and your playlists, liked songs, and settings remain untouched.

### ❓ What if Spotify updates and my modifications disappear?

That's exactly why the compatibility hotfix exists. Spotify frequently releases updates that can temporarily break modifications. With this fusion installer, the hotfix automatically patches any compatibility issues so your setup keeps working. If it doesn't auto-restore, simply run the installer again.

### ❓ Can I use this on Mac or Linux?

This installer is designed for **Windows** only. If you're on Mac or Linux, you'll need to install SpotX and Spicetify separately.

### ❓ My Spotify version is 1.2.x. Will this work?

Yes. The fusion installer explicitly supports Spotify 1.2.x (older stable versions) and 1.3.x+ with the new Rspack architecture. The compatibility hotfix detects your Spotify version and applies the correct patches.

### ❓ How do I remove this later?

If you ever want to go back to a plain Spotify, you can uninstall Spotify completely (Settings → Apps → Spotify → Uninstall), then reinstall it from spotify.com. Your account and playlists stay intact.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### 🔹 Spotify is open during installation

The installer closes and restarts Spotify automatically. Don't worry — your music or playlist is not affected.

### 🔹 Nothing seems to happen after running the installer

Wait 3-5 minutes. The installer works silently in the background. Check if Spotify appears in your taskbar or if a terminal window briefly opens and closes. If nothing happens after 5 minutes, try running the installer again.

### 🔹 Spotify shows an error after installation

Rare, but possible if Spotify was mid-update. Simply close Spotify, reopen it, and it should work. If not, uninstall Spotify, reinstall it, then run the fusion installer again.

### 🔹 Marketplace is missing from the sidebar

Open Spotify, click your profile picture → Settings → look for "Show Marketplace" or similar option, and enable it. If it's not there, re-run the installer.
